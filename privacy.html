About this app

HIIYAH (EarthNet) is the telecommunications app of OMNI-PRESENT SOLUTIONS, INC.: calling, messaging, and mobile data plans including eSIM plans (Unlimited eSIM, Travel eSIM, and Family Plan). eSIM activation is a demo until a real carrier partner connects.

What data the app handles

  • Demo accounts and plans: plan selections and account activity in this app are demo/session-only, held on your device for the session and not stored on our servers by this app.
  • No live telecom service yet: because no carrier partner is connected, the app does not collect the identity documents, payment details, or usage records a live carrier service would require.
  • No tracking: this app does not include third-party advertising trackers and does not sell personal data.
  • External links: links to the other Omni-Present Solutions apps and outside sites are covered by their own policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone.
  • We do not run third-party advertising networks or ad trackers in this app.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, call logs, or device identifiers for advertising.

Payments and financial features

Plan pricing shown is a demo preview. eSIM activation is demo only until a real carrier partner connects; no live plan is sold or activated through this app at this time and no payment is processed in it.

Data retention

Session-only demo data is cleared when the session ends.

Children

This app is directed to users 18 and over.
